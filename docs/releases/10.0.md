# Marque 10.0

**bloodhound 9.0.0 · trove 4.8.0 · guise 5.3.0 · disguise 5.3.0 · usarrs 8.5.0**

## What's new

**The ratio is enforced.** `min_ratio` has been in bloodhound's config since the start and
was never read. Now, under `ratio_mode` `full` (the default):

- A member who falls below it goes on **ratio watch**, with a deadline 14 days away.
- If they're still below at the deadline, **new downloading is paused** until they
  recover: a leech on a torrent they aren't already in is refused, and so is the `.torrent`.
- **Seeding is never paused**, because seeding is how a member recovers. A member can
  always finish what they'd started.
- It's judged hourly. A member who seeds back up is restored within the hour.

Members are protected in three ways:

- **A grace allowance:** nothing is enforced until a member has downloaded 5 GiB, so a new
  member's first download doesn't put them on watch.
- **Staff exemption:** moderators and above are exempt.
- **Per-member exemption:** an admin can exempt one member, with the reason on record:
  `bloodhound:standing exempt <id> --reason=...`.

Every number is configurable, and `ratio_mode` `off` enforces nothing.

**Members can see where they stand.** The dashboard's Tracker Stats panel, and
`/profile/stats`, say when a member is on watch (with the deadline and what to do), when
downloads are paused (and why), or that they're exempt. Events (`RatioWatchStarted`,
`MemberRestricted`, `StandingRestored`) are there to email them from.

**A malformed announce key gets a reason, not a 404.** This was opt-in in 9.1 and is now
the default.

## Who's affected

**Anyone running bloodhound.** Members already below 0.5 go on watch at the first hourly run
after you upgrade, and are paused 14 days later. Tell them before you upgrade, check the
numbers, exempt anyone who should be, or set `BLOODHOUND_RATIO_MODE=off`.

**Anyone whose frontend serves downloads (guise, disguise).** A refused download has to be
answered with a 403 and the reason, which guise and disguise do from 5.3. bloodhound 9
refuses to install beside older versions.

**A developer who builds downloads with trove directly.** `forDownload()` can now throw
`DownloadRefused`, and `NoAnnounceUrl` is one kind of it. Catch `DownloadRefused`.

**Public trackers (hound) and installs without a tracker:** nothing changes.

## What you need to do

Follow bloodhound's
[Upgrading from 8.x to 9.0](../../packages/bloodhound/README.md#upgrading-from-8x-to-90):

```bash
composer require marque/bloodhound:^9.0 marque/trove:^4.8 marque/guise:^5.3 marque/usarrs:^8.5 -W
php artisan migrate
```

Use `marque/disguise:^5.3` where that is your frontend. If you published usarrs' views,
re-publish `partials/tracker-figures.blade.php` to show members their standing.
