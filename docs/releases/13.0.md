# Marque 13.0 — 2026-11-11

**usarrs 10.0.0 · bosun 1.0.2 · marque 2.0.2**

## What's new

**Confirming it's you works for every member.** Before a sensitive change, usarrs asks the
member to confirm it's them (Laravel's `password.confirm`). The page used to ask only for a
password. On a site that signs in with OAuth, a member's password is random and nobody
knows it, and on a magic-link site the member never chose one. So for those members,
anything behind the confirmation (managing passkeys, for a start) was a dead end. The page
now offers whatever the member actually has:

- their password, where passwords sign in;
- signing in again with an OAuth provider connected to their account;
- a passkey;
- a one-time link to their verified email address. They can approve it from any device,
  and the page it opens names the browser that asked.

**The confirmation now guards more.** Connecting or removing an OAuth provider, turning
two-factor on or off, seeing or regenerating recovery codes, and changing the email
address or password all ask for it. Before, a stolen session could do any of these: connect
the thief's own provider and keep a way in after the owner took the account back, read the
recovery codes, or turn on two-factor with the thief's authenticator and lock the owner out.

**Members can see and remove their OAuth connections.** On an OAuth site the profile lists
the member's connected providers, with Connect and Remove. Each change mails the member a
notice, and removing the last way to sign in is refused.

**Remember-me is a choice.** Every OAuth and magic-link sign-in used to set a long-lived
"remember me" cookie. It's now a checkbox, off by default, as on the password form.

**Invites can't be planted, and the invite email works on OAuth sites.** An invite used to
travel in a link any page could trigger, so another site could slip its own invite into a
visitor's sign-up. On an OAuth site the invite email's own link went to a 404. `/register`
is now the invite's landing page there, and the invite travels only with its button.

**Also:**
- A magic-link sign-in counts as verifying the address it was sent to.
- On an install under `usarrs.prefix`, emailed magic links 404'd. They work now.
- Several low-severity OAuth fixes from an earlier review: mail limits, provider data that
  failed to save, race conditions that showed an error page.

## Who's affected

**Sites on the `socialite` (OAuth) or `magic_link` drivers**, most of all. Their members get
a confirm page that works, a connected-accounts section, and no remember-me unless they ask
for it.

**Anyone who published usarrs' views.** Six changed, and old copies of some break the new
features. The list is in usarrs'
[Upgrading from 9.x to 10.0](../../packages/usarrs/README.md#upgrading-from-9x-to-100).

**Anyone with their own links into OAuth.** `?invite=` on the OAuth redirect is ignored, and
a signed-in member's OAuth round trip connects nothing unless it started from their profile.

**Every site that sends usarrs mail.** The connection link, the confirmation link and the
connection notices are queued.

## What you need to do

```bash
composer require marque/usarrs:^10.0 -W
```

bosun and the installer accept usarrs 10 from 1.0.2 and 2.0.2. Then work through usarrs'
[Upgrading from 9.x to 10.0](../../packages/usarrs/README.md#upgrading-from-9x-to-100):
republish any views you published, point your own invite links at `/register?invite=CODE`,
and run a queue worker (or the `sync` connection).
