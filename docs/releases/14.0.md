# Marque 14.0 — 2026-11-15

**usarrs 11.0.0 · bosun 1.0.3 · marque 2.0.3**

## What's new

**Only admins and the owner can create invites, by default.** With invites switched on,
every signed-in member used to be able to create them, and admins and the owner had the
same allowance as everyone else. Nobody had decided that. On a private tracker, invites
are how you control who joins, and handing that to every member, including one who joined
yesterday, defeats the point of `invite_only`.

- **`invites.min_role`** sets the lowest role that can create invites: `user`, `uploader`,
  `moderator`, `admin` (the default) or `owner`. An owner counts as an admin.
- **`invites.max_per_role`** sets allowances by role. By default admins and the owner
  have no limit, and everyone else keeps `invites.max_per_user`.
- **One rule decides it everywhere.** The create page, the create button and the
  dashboard's Invites panel all follow the same policy, so a member who can't invite isn't
  offered it. A member who loses the right, through a demotion say, can still see and
  revoke what they already sent.
- **An expired invite no longer uses up an allowance.** It used to hold its slot until
  someone revoked it.

## Also since 13.0: usarrs 10.1 (2026-11-14)

usarrs 10.1, a minor, already limits sign-up and the mail a visitor can make your site
send: 5 accounts an hour per IP, sign-in and reset links per address and per IP, and
verification resends per member. It also lets you put a human check, such as Cloudflare
Turnstile, on register, login and password reset. If you skipped 10.1, its notes are in
usarrs' [CHANGELOG](../../packages/usarrs/CHANGELOG.md).

## Who's affected

**Sites with invites switched on, where members invite each other.** After upgrading,
only admins and the owner can create invites. Members who could before get a 403 on
`/invites/create`, and the button and panel stop offering it.

**Anyone who published `config/usarrs.php`.** Your `invites` block won't have the new keys.
usarrs falls back to the shipped defaults (admins and the owner create invites, with no
limit), so nothing breaks,
but add them to your copy to see and change them.

**Anyone who published the dashboard's Invites panel view.** The new copy says "No limit"
for admins and the owner; an old copy shows a blank count.

## What you need to do

```bash
composer require marque/usarrs:^11.0 -W
```

bosun and the installer accept usarrs 11 from 1.0.3 and 2.0.3.

**To keep letting every member invite**, set `USARRS_INVITES_MIN_ROLE=user` (or
`uploader` for uploaders and above). Otherwise there's nothing to do. The rest is in
usarrs' [Upgrading from 10.x to 11.0](../../packages/usarrs/README.md#upgrading-from-10x-to-110).
